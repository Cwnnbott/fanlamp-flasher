🌀 FanLamp Bridge — flash your ESP32

This installs the FanLamp Bridge firmware, which lets Clover control your FanLamp Pro ceiling fan over the internet.

1 Plug the ESP32 into your PC with a data USB cable (some cables are charge-only).
2 Open this page in Google Chrome (WebSerial doesn't work in other browsers), then click install:

Pick the USB serial port (e.g. USB-SERIAL / CP210x / CH340). If nothing appears, try another cable or USB port.
3 After flashing, on your phone join the WiFi network FanLampBridge, and a setup page will pop up. Enter:
  • Your home WiFi name + password — must be the 2.4 GHz network (ESP32 can't see 5 GHz)
  • MQTT server: broker.hivemq.com, port 1883 (already filled in)
Then hit Save. The ESP32 will reboot and connect.
4 Tell Clover it's online — she'll take it from there (learning your fan's ID, then a live test).
